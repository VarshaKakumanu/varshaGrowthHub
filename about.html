It started with my own career

I started as a web developer and spent years learning, working through challenges and understanding what it takes to grow. Over five years in IT, I saw how much practical learning, clear communication and good guidance matter, and how much it helps to learn from people who have already walked the path.

Career growth isn't only about finishing courses or adding technologies. It's about understanding how the industry works, explaining your ideas with confidence, building meaningful projects and finding the right people to learn from.

You don't need to spend tens of thousands of rupees to take your next step. Sometimes the right mentor, an honest resume review or a supportive peer group is enough.

Once I reached a stable position in a multinational company, I wanted to make that journey easier for others. Rooting Careers brings people, knowledge and opportunities together in one community where you can ask questions without hesitation, in your own language, and grow at a pace that suits you.
